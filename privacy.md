# Privacy Policy for Jyovana

Jyovana was earlier called Jyotirmala. Only the name has changed.

**Version 1.0** · **Effective date: [publish date]** · **Last updated: 30 October 2026**

This policy covers the Android app **Jyovana** (package `com.atharvaapps.jyovana`). It is published by
Harsha Bharadwaj, an individual developer trading as Atharva Apps ("Atharva Apps", "we", "us").

## The short version

- Your birth details stay on your phone, and all calculations run on your phone.
  The app has no account and no sign-in, and we keep no copy on a server. We do not receive your
  birth details.

- The app includes Google's advertising SDK. It may contact Google when the app starts and send
  your advertising ID and other device data described below. The app shows banner, full-screen and
  rewarded ads served by this SDK. Google never receives your birth details.

- The app has no analytics and no crash reporting of its own.

- You can delete your data on the phone at any time.

## 1. What the app keeps on your phone

When you add a person, the app saves:

- name and gender
- date and time of birth
- place of birth (name, latitude/longitude and time zone)
- how the person relates to you (me, spouse, child or other), and up to three "My Day" focus areas.

The app also saves:

- the place you choose for the daily panchāṅga
- your calculation and display settings
- your thumbs-up/down votes on "My Day" (if you give any)
- the 24-hour unlocks you earned by watching an ad.

All of this sits in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it, because
Android keeps each app's storage separate. The app does not add its own encryption on top of
Android's.

Charts and readings are calculated on your phone each time you open them. They are not saved.

The app finds places from a list built into the app, or from coordinates you type. It does not
send your search to any service.

## 1A. Details of other people you add
If you save another person's birth details (for example, a spouse, child, parent or friend), please do so only with their permission, or as their parent or guardian. These details are kept only on your phone for your personal and family use. We do not receive them. You are responsible for sharing them only as that person would expect, including when you use "Export people".

## 2. What leaves your phone

**The app sends us nothing.** It makes no connection to any server of ours. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and we use it only to reply to you and keep a record of the request. We delete it within 12 months unless the law requires us to keep it longer. The app uses no
location-lookup or time-zone web service, and it loads no fonts or files from the internet.

Data leaves your phone only in these cases:

1. **Advertising (Google).** See section 3.

2. **When you export.** "Export people (JSON)" in Settings opens Android's share menu with your
   saved people and votes. You choose where it goes (for example email, a chat app or a drive), and
   that app's own privacy policy then applies.

3. **Android backup.** Depending on your phone's settings, Android may copy app data to your
   Google account or to a new phone during transfer. You can turn this off in your phone's backup
   settings.

4. **Links you open.** For example, the licence link in Settings opens in your browser.

## 3. Advertising and your advertising ID

The app includes the **Google Mobile Ads SDK** (Google AdMob).

The app shows banner, full-screen and rewarded ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK and may contact Google when the app starts. Rewarded ads are optional: you can choose to watch one to unlock a premium report for 24 hours. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the SDK collects the data listed below (including your advertising ID, IP address, the approximate location Google infers from it, and how you interact with the app and its ads) and sends it to Google. Your birth details, names and places are never shared with Google. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google asks for your consent, and you can change it under Settings → Ad Privacy Choices. Everywhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

The SDK and the Android advertising ID
(`AD_ID`) permission are still part of the app, so its Google Play listing says "Contains ads".

Google says the SDK collects, by default:

- your device's **IP address**. Google may use it to infer your approximate location
- **app interactions**, such as app launches, taps and video views
- **diagnostic information**, such as app launch time, hangs and energy use
- **device and account identifiers**: the Android **advertising ID**, the **app set ID** and, if
  applicable, identifiers related to Google accounts signed in on the device.

Google uses this data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and sends it encrypted
(TLS). Google may use it to show personalised ads. Google's use is covered by Google's own privacy
policy: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>.

**The app never gives Google your name, birth details or places.** With each ad request the app
passes only the ad slot's identifier. Google's SDK adds the device data listed above.

**Your choices:**

- The app shows banner, full-screen and rewarded ads. You choose whether to watch a rewarded ad.
  In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, you can refuse consent and still use the app.

- In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google asks for your consent for advertising. You can change your
  answer at any time under **Settings → Ad Privacy Choices**.

- You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (on most phones:
  **Settings → Google → Ads** or **Settings → Privacy → Ads**).

## 4. Notifications

This version of the app does not send notifications and does not ask for notification permission.

## 5. Permissions

The app does not ask for your location permission, camera, microphone, contacts, phone or calendar.
It does not ask for notification permission either. Internet access is used only by the advertising SDK. The advertising-ID permission
is also used only by the advertising SDK. The app does not use your phone's location, but Google
may infer your approximate location from your IP address (section 3).

## 6. No analytics, no selling

The app has no analytics or crash-reporting service of its own. We do not sell your data. Your
birth details, names and places never leave your phone and are not shared with anyone. **The ad
data in section 3 is shared with Google**, through the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app, for
advertising, analytics and fraud prevention.

## 7. How long data is kept and how to delete it

- The data the app keeps (section 1) stays on your phone until you delete it. Ad data the SDK sends to Google (section 3) is held by Google, as described below.

- **Delete one person:** open the person and choose Delete.

- **Delete everything:** clear the app's storage in Android settings (**Settings → Apps →
  Jyovana → Storage → Clear storage**), or uninstall the app. This removes every person,
  setting, vote and unlock on the phone. If Android backup is on for your phone, a backup copy may
  remain in your Google account (section 2, point 3).

- Data held by Google for advertising follows Google's retention rules. You can reset your
  advertising ID as described in section 3.

## 8. Children
Jyovana is meant only for adults (18 and over). It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly allow anyone under 18 to use it. If you are under 18, please do not use the app. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has used it, write to us (section 12) and we will help you remove the data from the device and explain how to ask Google to delete advertising data linked to that device. We cannot delete data held by Google ourselves.
You can save birth details of family members, including children, for your own personal and family use. These details stay only on your phone. We never receive them, and they are never passed to the advertising SDK.

## 9. Your rights

**India (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023):** You have the right to (a) a summary of your personal data and how it is processed, (b) correction, completion, updating and erasure, (c) grievance redress, and (d) nominating another person to exercise these rights if you die or become incapable. Because the app keeps your birth details only on your phone and we never receive them, you can use most of these rights directly in the app: view or edit a person, delete a person, or clear the app's storage (section 7). For anything else, including data the ads SDK sends to Google, write to our Grievance Officer (section 12). Where you gave consent, you can withdraw it as easily as you gave it. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, use Settings → Ad Privacy Choices. Everywhere, you can choose not to watch rewarded ads, reset or delete your advertising ID, or uninstall the app. Withdrawal does not affect processing that happened before it. We will reply within 10 working days (see section 12). If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, after first using our grievance contact as that Act requires.

**European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland (GDPR, UK GDPR and Swiss FADP):** we do not receive your personal data.
Google asks for your consent for advertising (section 3). You can withdraw or change your consent at any time
under Settings → Ad Privacy Choices. For data Google holds, you can contact Google
using the link in section 3.

## 10. Security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage, protected by Android's app sandbox and your
phone's own encryption and screen lock. Data the advertising SDK sends to Google is encrypted in
transit, according to Google.

## 11. Changes to this policy

If the app starts collecting anything new (for example sign-in, payments or a server), we will
update this page and change the effective date before that version is released.
