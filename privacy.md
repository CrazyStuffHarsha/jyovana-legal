# Privacy Policy for Jyovana

Jyovana was earlier called Jyotirmala. Only the name has changed.

**Version 1.1** · **Effective date: 11 November 2026** · **Last updated: 10 November 2026**

This policy covers the Android app **Jyovana** (package `com.atharvaapps.jyovana`). It is published by
Harsha Bharadwaj, an individual developer trading as Atharva Apps ("Atharva Apps", "we", "us").

## The short version

- Your birth details stay on your phone, and all calculations run on your phone.
  The app has no account and no sign-in, and we keep no copy on a server. We do not receive your
  birth details.

- This version of the app shows no ads. It does not include any advertising SDK and does not use your advertising ID.

- The app has no analytics and no crash reporting of its own.

- You can delete your data on the phone at any time.

## 1. What the app keeps on your phone

When you add a person, the app saves:

- name and gender
- date and time of birth
- place of birth (name, latitude/longitude and time zone)
- how the person relates to you (me, spouse, child or other), and up to three "My Day" focus areas.

The app also saves:

- the place you choose for the daily panchanga
- your calculation and display settings
- your thumbs-up/down votes on "My Day" (if you give any)

All of this sits in the app's private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it, because
Android keeps each app's storage separate. The app does not add its own encryption on top of
Android's.

Charts and readings are calculated on your phone. Some results may be kept on your phone so they load faster. They never leave your phone, and "Delete all my data" removes them.

The app finds places from a list built into the app, or from coordinates you type. It does not
send your search to any service.

## 1A. Details of other people you add
If you save another person's birth details (for example, a spouse, child, parent or friend), please do so only with their permission, or as their parent or guardian. These details are kept only on your phone for your personal and family use. We do not receive them. You are responsible for sharing them only as that person would expect, including when you use "Save a backup file".

## 2. What leaves your phone

**The app sends us nothing.** It makes no connection to any server of ours. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and we use it only to reply to you and keep a record of the request. We delete it within 12 months unless the law requires us to keep it longer. The app uses no
location-lookup or time-zone web service, and it loads no fonts or files from the internet.

Data leaves your phone only in these cases:

1. **Ads.** This version of the app shows no ads and has no advertising SDK. See section 3.

2. **When you export.** "Save a backup file" in Settings opens Android's share menu with your
   saved people and votes. You choose where it goes (for example email, a chat app or a drive), and
   that app's own privacy policy then applies.

3. **Android backup.** The app opts out of Android backup. The data it saves on your phone is not
   included in your phone's Google backups, and it is not copied to a new phone during
   device-to-device transfer.

4. **Links you open.** For example, the licence link in Settings opens in your browser.

## 3. Ads
This version of the app shows no ads. It does not include any advertising SDK and does not use your advertising ID. If a later version adds ads, we will update this policy before that version is released. We will say which ad provider is used and what data it collects, and you will be able to choose whether to watch rewarded ads.

## 4. Notifications

This version of the app does not send notifications and does not ask for notification permission.

## 5. Permissions

The app does not ask for your location permission, camera, microphone, contacts, phone or calendar.
It does not ask for notification permission either. The advertising-ID permission is not used by this version.

## 6. No analytics, no selling

The app has no analytics or crash-reporting service of its own. We do not sell your data. Your
birth details, names and places never leave your phone and are not shared with anyone.

## 7. How long data is kept and how to delete it

- The data the app keeps (section 1) stays on your phone until you delete it.

- **Delete one person:** open the person and choose Delete.

- **Delete everything:** in the app, open **Settings, then Delete all my data**. This removes every person, setting and vote saved by the app on your phone. Uninstalling the app also removes this data. Because the app opts out of Android backup, no backup copy of this data remains in your Google account (section 2, point 3).

## 8. Children
Jyovana is meant only for adults (18 and over). It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly allow anyone under 18 to use it. If you are under 18, please do not use the app. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has used it, write to us (section 12) and we will help you remove the data from the device.
You can save birth details of family members, including children, for your own personal and family use. These details stay only on your phone. We never receive them, and they never leave your phone.

## 9. Your rights

**India (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023):** You have the right to (a) a summary of your personal data and how it is processed, (b) correction, completion, updating and erasure, (c) grievance redress, and (d) nominating another person to exercise these rights if you die or become incapable. Because the app keeps your birth details only on your phone and we never receive them, you can use most of these rights directly in the app: view or edit a person, delete a person, or use Settings, then Delete all my data (section 7). For anything else, write to our Grievance Officer (section 12). Where you gave consent, you can withdraw it as easily as you gave it. Withdrawal does not affect processing that happened before it. We will reply within 10 working days (see section 12). If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, after first using our grievance contact as that Act requires.

**European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland (GDPR, UK GDPR and Swiss FADP):** we do not receive your personal data.

## 10. Security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage, protected by Android's app sandbox and your
phone's own encryption and screen lock.

## 11. Changes to this policy

If the app starts collecting anything new (for example sign-in, payments or a server), we will
update this page and change the effective date before that version is released.
